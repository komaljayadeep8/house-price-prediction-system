FAQ Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Customer Care Questions

How accurate is the UrbanValuate price estimate?

The appraisal is generated by an empirically benchmarked Ridge Regression model trained on 545 verified metropolitan home sales, achieving an R² of 0.6590 and MAE of ₹9.60 Lakhs. It accounts for carpet area, rooms, ensuite parity, road access, and micro-market baseline rates.

Can I request an official physical survey?

Yes. You can submit an inquiry above or contact our advisory desk directly. Our senior chartered surveyor desk can arrange on-site structural inspections and bank-approved appraisal documentation.

How is the 5-year capital growth calculated?

Growth projections use micro-market compound annual growth rates (CAGR) derived from historical transaction velocity, commercial IT absorption, and transit infrastructure maturity (e.g. 10.8% in Cyber City, 8.5% in Downtown Central).

Can I print or export my valuation certificate?

Yes! On the Valuation Studio page, simply click Print Certificate or Save Full Dossier (PDF). The system contains an optimized print stylesheet formatted specifically for presentation dossiers.
